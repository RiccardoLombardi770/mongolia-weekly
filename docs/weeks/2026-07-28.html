Rio Tinto's Oyu Tolgoi copper mine has resumed exports following a blockade in Mongolia, Mining.com reported. The disruption had halted shipments from one of the country's largest foreign-invested projects, underscoring operational risks facing large-scale mining investment even as the mine remains central to Mongolia's export revenue base. The Mongolia-China cross-border railway is 70 to 80 per cent complete and is scheduled for completion this year, the Mongolian Mining Journal reported; the same outlet reported that the two countries have separately approved a second rail-link agreement, nearly 70 years after the first such accord. Mongolia's parliament, the State Great Khural, has selected a new prime minister amid ongoing corruption allegations, the South China Morning Post reported. The appointment follows a period of political instability in which the previous government lost the confidence of parliament, part of a wider pattern of leadership turnover tied to public frustration over graft.Монголын парламент болох Төрийн Их Хурал үргэлжилсэн ташаа авалтын үзэгдлүүдийн дунд шинэ сайдыг сонгон шалгаруулсан гэж South China Morning Post мэдээлэв. Энэхүү томилгоо өмнөх засгийн газар парламентийн итгэлийг алдсан үеийн дараа гарсан бөгөөд энэ нь ташаа авалтын дээж өндөр төрийн сэтгэл ухаалгаас шалтгаалсан удирдлагын эргүүлэлтийн үргэлжилсэн хэв маяг юм.

Mongolia is scheduled to host the 17th session of the Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD COP17) in Ulaanbaatar from 17 to 28 August 2026. The summit is expected to draw international attention to land degradation, desertification and drought resilience issues of direct relevance to Mongolia's pastoral economy.
